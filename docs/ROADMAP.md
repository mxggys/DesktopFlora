# Roadmap

Wishes noted for later, in the order they came.

## Look and feel: from good to real

Go through every kind one at a time and make it look as real as it can, so it feels like it is really raining or
snowing on the screen. Rain first.

- Depth: a far layer (small, faint, slow, a little blurred), a middle one, and a rare near one (big, blurred, fast).
- Light from the wallpaper: what falls takes the colour and brightness of what is behind it.
- Motion blur as a camera sees it: thin, faint streaks rather than drops.
- Unevenness: showers coming and going, gusts, pauses.
- The glass as a lens: drops that bend and mirror what is behind them.
- Less is more: rather fainter and sparser than a full screen.
- Better shaders throughout.

Kinds to go through: snow, autumn leaves, rain, cherry blossoms, thunderstorm, fog, hail, sleet, freezing rain,
graupel, autumn storm, blossom wind.
