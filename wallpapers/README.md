# Wallpapers

Desktop pictures made for DesktopFlora, drawn by the Python scripts next to them (NumPy and Pillow) rather than painted.

- `bergsee-nacht-2560x1440.png`: a mountain lake at night under the moon and the stars, the ranges mirrored in the
  water, mist over the shore. Drawn by `bergsee.py`.
- `herbstabend-2560x1440.png`: a warm autumn evening, deep brown into rust and amber, soft lights out of focus,
  leaves drifting down and lying in a heap along the bottom. Drawn by `herbstabend.py`; goes with the *Cozy* mood.

Set one as your desktop picture in Windows: right-click it, then *Set as desktop background*. Under a picture like
this the snow and rain pick up its light, and the drops on the glass show it bent.
